Save Data on Any Device

Route your phone, laptop, or browser through DataSaver Pro

Set Windows to automatically route network traffic through DataSaver Pro:

Option A: Automatic PAC URL (Recommended) Settings > Network & Internet > Proxy > Use setup script > Turn On.
Option B: Manual Proxy Manual proxy setup > Proxy IP: 127.0.0.1 Port: 8888

Connect your Android phone or tablet on the same WiFi:

  1. Open Settings > Wi-Fi on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the gear / settings icon next to your connected Wi-Fi network.
  3. Tap Proxy and select Auto-config (PAC).
  4. Enter the PAC URL:

Connect your iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open Settings > Wi-Fi.
  2. Tap the blue (i) info button next to your Wi-Fi name.
  3. Scroll to the bottom to Configure Proxy > select Automatic.
  4. Paste the PAC URL in the URL field:

You can also load the built-in DataSaver Extension in Chrome, Edge, or Brave:

  1. Go to chrome://extensions in your browser.
  2. Enable Developer mode (toggle in top-right).
  3. Click Load unpacked.
  4. Select the extension folder in this project!
The extension will immediately enforce Save-Data: on headers and suppress video autoplay across all browser tabs.
