Route your phone, laptop, or browser through DataSaver Pro

Set Windows to automatically route network traffic through DataSaver Pro:

Automatic PAC URL (Recommended) Settings > Network & Internet > Proxy > Use setup script > Turn On.

Connect your Android device:

  1. Open Settings > Wi-Fi.
  2. Tap the gear next to your connected Wi-Fi > Proxy > Auto-config (PAC).
  3. Enter the PAC URL:

Connect your iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open Settings > Wi-Fi > tap the blue (i) next to your Wi-Fi name.
  2. Scroll to Configure Proxy > choose Automatic.
  3. Enter the PAC URL:

Install the DataSaver Pro Extension by Ad3n1x in Chrome, Edge, or Brave:

  1. Go to chrome://extensions in your browser.
  2. Enable Developer mode (top-right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extension folder in this repo.
